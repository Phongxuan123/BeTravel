Announcement

Application Expiry for “New Application” and “Pending Payment” Status

28-Sep-2026

Please be informed that, in accordance with the implementation of the Immigration Department of Malaysia's new visa application policy,
**effective 1 October 2026**, any application in
**“New Application”** or **“Pending Payment”** status for more than
**7 working days** will automatically expire and be removed from the **“My Application(s)”** list. This policy applies to all applications, including those created prior to 1 September 2026.

If your application expires, you must submit a new application using the updated visa application form.

Thank you for your attention and cooperation.

Announcement

Multiple Entry Visa (MEV)

18-Sep-2026

**Effective 1 September 2026**, Multiple Entry Visa (MEV) will no longer be available for all nationalities and will be limited to **31 eligible nationalities** for the following purposes:

i. Fly & Cruise
ii. Business
iii. Medical Treatment
iv. Wedding Tourism

Announcement

ACTIVATION OF PHOTO VALIDATION FOR eVISA VDR APPLICATIONS

28-Sep-2026

Please be informed that, **effective 1 July 2026**, the Photo Validation feature will be activated for all eVISA applications under the VDR ( **Visa with Reference**) category submitted through the MyVISA Portal. All photographs uploaded via malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my will undergo an automated validation process to ensure compliance with the Malaysia Visa Photo Specifications.

Permitted Background Colours:

\- **White** or
\- **Blue**

The background must be plain and uniform, with no gradients, patterns, textures, or objects. Shadows or reflections on the background are not permitted. Any photograph that fails the validation process must be referred to the VDR Issuing Office. This notice applies only to eVISA applications under the VDR ( **Visa with Reference**) category submitted through the MyVISA Portal at malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my.

Announcement

Important Notice for Myanmar Passport Holders

18-Sep-2026

Malaysia will implement the ASEAN Framework Agreement on Visa Exemption\*. Under this framework, Myanmar passport holders traveling to Malaysia for social visits will be allowed visa-free entry for up to 14 days, effective 1 October 2025.

Applicants who have already obtained a visa will be entitled to stay in Malaysia for up to 30 days.

Please note that we are unable to offer refunds for any visa fees that have already been paid.

Announcement

Register MDAC Before Entering Malaysia

28-Sep-2026

All foreign nationals are **REQUIRED** to register the Malaysia Digital Arrival Card ( **MDAC**) within 3 days before arriving in Malaysia. Passport must be valid for at least six (6) months from the date of entry. Please refer to the link below this portal to complete your registration.

Passport Photo Requirements

Before you submit your application, make sure your passport-sized photo complies with the specification below. Non-compliant photos will lead to rejection of your application.

![](https://malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my/icon/tick.svg)

#### Full face, front view, with eyes open and a neutral expression.

![](https://malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my/icon/tick.svg)

#### No head covering, except for religious reasons — the face must remain fully visible.

![](https://malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my/icon/tick.svg)

#### VTR Application: Plain white background, with no border or frame around the photo.

![](https://malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my/icon/tick.svg)

#### VDR Application: Plain white or plain blue background, with no border or frame around the photo.

![](https://malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my/icon/tick.svg)

#### Photo size of 35mm (width) × 50mm (height).

![](https://malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my/icon/tick.svg)

#### Head height of 30–35mm, measured from chin to crown.

![](https://malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my/icon/tick.svg)

#### Top margin of about 5mm and a chin margin of 5–10mm.

![](https://malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my/icon/tick.svg)

#### Image must be clear and sharp — not blurred, tilted, too far, or too small.
