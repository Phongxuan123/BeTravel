# MOSQUE TOUR PROGRAMME

**Assalamualaikum (May peace be upon you).****Welcome to the Mosque Tour Programme (MTP).**

MTP at Masjid Wilayah Persekutuan aims to facilitate our local and international tourists during their visit to the mosque.

We offer free guided tours of approximately 45 minutes, covering areas of interest such as the history and architecture of the mosque and overview of Islam. There are plenty of good photo spots throughout the tour. If you wish, you can even observe the congregational prayer (at around 1 pm) as this is one of the few mosques in Malaysia that offers visitors a chance to do so.

For ease of direction, we advise visitors to arrive at Anjung B entrance (Door B) where you will be greeted by our personnel and be given robes and headscarves. You will then be assigned to a guide who will be taking you on a journey around our magnificent mosque.

Subject to weather conditions, visitors will also be brought to the outer foyer of the mosque for further photo opportunities. As these areas are exposed to the sun, we advise visitors to wear socks to help protect the feet from the heat.

We look forward to seeing you soon and wish you a pleasant visit.

**Visit Checklist:**

\- For group visits, please submit your request to (emel : [masjidwp@jawi.gov.my](mailto:masjidwp@jawi.gov.my)). The administrator will review it and inform you once it is approved. Please note that the group visit is suitable for participants who are 11 years old and above.

\- Please arrange your arrival at Anjung B (Door B)

\- Please allocate at least 45 minutes for the tour

\- Please bring socks for comfort
