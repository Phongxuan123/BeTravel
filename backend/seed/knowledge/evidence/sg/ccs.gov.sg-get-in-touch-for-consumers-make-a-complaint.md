# Make a Complaint

Provides consumers with a structured process for filing complaints, outlining submission requirements, investigation procedures, and resolution mechanisms to ensure fair competition and consumer protection in Singapore.

Last updated on 8 May 2026

## Making a Complaint to CCS

CCS administers the Consumer Protection (Fair Trading) Act (" **CPFTA**") and Competition Act.

**Consumer Protection Matters**

CCS does not assist consumers in disputes with suppliers nor in obtaining redress. Under the [CPFTA](https://www.ccs.gov.sg/consumer-protection/fair-trading-practices/about-fair-trading-practices/), the Consumers Association of Singapore (“ [**CASE** ↗ (opens in new tab)](http://www.case.org.sg/consumer_guides_cpfta.aspx)”) and Singapore Tourism Board (“ [**STB** ↗ (opens in new tab)](https://www.stb.gov.sg/contact-us/)”) remain the first points of contact for local consumers and tourists respectively to handle complaints. CASE will assist aggrieved consumers to obtain redress, and in some cases, compensation through negotiation and/or mediation. For assistance, please approach CASE at 6277 5100 or visit [www.case.org.sg ↗ (opens in new tab)](http://www.case.org.sg/).

If you are a supplier and have issue with another supplier, you may wish to seek your own legal advice or bring your case to the Small Claims Tribunal (“ **SCT**”). More information on bringing a case to the SCT can be found [here ↗ (opens in new tab)](https://www.judiciary.gov.sg/civil/small-claims).

**Competition Matters**

If you suspect that any business, company or organisation is engaged in anti-competitive conduct that infringes the Competition Act, please file a complaint with CCS.

Complaints can be lodged if you believe there has been a breach of any of the following prohibitions under the Competition Act:

- [Anti-competitive agreements, decisions or concerted practices](https://www.ccs.gov.sg/anti-competitive-practices/agreements-and-collaborations/) (e.g. price fixing, bid rigging, market sharing, output limitation, etc)

- [Abuse of a dominant position](https://www.ccs.gov.sg/anti-competitive-practices/dominance-and-conduct/) (e.g. predatory pricing, tying, refusal to supply, etc); or

- [Mergers and acquisitions that substantially lessen competition](https://www.ccs.gov.sg/anti-competitive-practices/mergers-and-acquisitions/) (e.g. two close competitors with significant market shares merged, etc)


Complaints may not be pursued if they fall within the specific exclusions, for which CCS has no powers to conduct an investigation. For more information on exclusions from the Competition Act, please [click here](https://www.ccs.gov.sg/anti-competitive-practices/legislation-and-guidelines/exclusions-and-exemptions/).

Before making a complaint, please note the following:

**General**

CCS does not provide legal advice nor views on hypothetical scenarios.

As the administering agency of the CPFTA and Competition Act, it is not appropriate for CCS to advise you on the application of the law. You may wish to seek your own legal advice.

If the subject matter of your complaint does not fall within the scope of the prohibitions under the Competition Act, the CPFTA, or CCS does not have the powers to conduct an investigation into the subject matter of your complaint, we may redirect you to the relevant government agencies. ## Preparing to File a Complaint with CCS

To file a complaint, you should have the following information ready:

- Information about you and the organisation you represent (if applicable);

- Information about the party or parties involved;

- A brief description of the unfair practice, agreement, abusive conduct or merger that you are complaining about;

- Any other relevant information and supporting documents.
