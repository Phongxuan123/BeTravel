# Requirements for flying unmanned aircraft

Learn about the rules and requirements for operating unmanned aircraft safely and responsibly in Singapore.

Last updated on 8 May 2026

To ensure unmanned aircraft (UA) operations are safe and responsible, UA users must meet the relevant regulatory requirements before flying UAs in Singapore.

## Who must comply with these requirements

These requirements apply to anyone flying a UA in Singapore, including Singapore Citizens, Singapore Permanent Residents, and non-residents.

The UA regulatory framework has 3 classifications of UA uses:

- recreational

- educational

- business

Observe these safety guidelines when operating your UA.

![Flying DON'Ts - safety guidelines when operating your unmanned aircraft](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/175/8ed3df71-d8af-4b5b-b286-e441036b142a/image_2.png)

Download the [Flying Dos and Don’ts. ↗ (opens in new tab)](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/175/763c234b-ccc3-4539-a20e-2a97c57ffb95/ua-safety-guidelines.pdf)

When you fly a UA, make sure you understand the regulatory requirements, follow the rules, and observe the safety guidelines. CAAS takes enforcement action against those who do not comply to keep our skies safe.

For details on the requirements, rules and penalties, refer to:

- [Air Navigation (101 Unmanned Aircraft Operations) Regulations](https://www.caas.gov.sg/resources/legislation/air-navigation-act/)

- [Advisory circulars and safety bulletins on UA regulations](https://www.caas.gov.sg/resources/regulatory-requirements-and-guidelines/unmanned-aircraft/)


To fly your UA, including drones, across borders, please refer to advisory circular [AC 101-2-3 Permits for unmanned aircraft operations ↗ (opens in new tab)](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/175/a26d2343-f895-4d4e-a4df-d715ae9fd8f8/ac-anr101-2-3-cross-border-permits-for-unmanned-aircraft-(final).pdf).
