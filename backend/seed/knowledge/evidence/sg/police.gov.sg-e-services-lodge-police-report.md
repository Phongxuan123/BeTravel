Content Overview

You can use this e-service to make a police report for a crime that does not require immediate police action such as a theft or a scam. If you are reporting a scam, please refer to  [ScamShield Website](https://www.scamshield.gov.sg/important-information-for-police-report/) for information to include in your police report.

For Government Users, please log in via [https://go.gov.sg/police-report-login-gov](https://go.gov.sg/police-report-login-gov)

1.1 How to Report Other Types of Crime

**Emergency** – call 999 or SMS 70999

**Traffic Offense** – please lodge a [Traffic Accident Report](https://police.gov.sg/E-Services/Lodge-Traffic-Accident-Report) instead

**Missing Persons** – call 999 or visit a Neighbourhood Police Centre to make a report

**Loss/theft of NRIC or Singapore Passport** – no police report is required. Refer to the ICA website [https://www.ica.gov.sg](https://www.ica.gov.sg/)

1.2 What You Need

- Serial number and photo of the missing item (if applicable)
- Supporting documents
  - Accepted file formats: BMP, DOC, DOCX, JIF, JPG, JPEG, PDF, PNG, PPT, TXT, XLS, XLSX.

  - File size limit: 10MB x 5 files
